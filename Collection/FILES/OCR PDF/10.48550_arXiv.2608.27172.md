C. Training
Thetrainingandvalidationsetsarecomposedexclusivelyof
normal samples, accounting for 70% and 20% of the normal
data, respectively. The test set consists of the remaining 10%
of normal samples combined with all anomalous samples.
A batch size of 50 is used for ModernBERT-large and 12
for SmolLM2-360M, with values chosen to maximize the
utilization of the available VRAM on an NVIDIA RTX 3090
GPU.Duetomemoryconstraints,gradientaccumulationsteps
are employed to stabilize the training process. Early stopping
is applied based on validation loss, halting training when
performance on the validation set begins to degrade.
Additionalimplementationdetailsdifferacrossexperiments
but do not impact the explainability analysis. A complete
specification of all training parameters is provided in the
accompanying code repository [16].
